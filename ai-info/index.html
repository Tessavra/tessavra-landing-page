What is Tessavra?

Tessavra is a conversation and interaction intelligence platform for enterprise customer support, quality assurance, customer experience and research teams.

It transforms recorded customer interactions into structured, searchable and evidence-backed intelligence. Teams can use Tessavra to review conversations, evaluate service quality, identify recurring customer issues, coach employees and deliver validated insights into operational systems.

Tessavra is more than a transcription tool. Its capabilities include interaction analysis, quality management, coaching, voice-of-customer intelligence, research analysis, semantic search, governed AI workflows and enterprise data pipelines.

Core capabilities

Conversation intelligence

Transcribe, structure, search and analyse recorded customer interactions.

Interaction review

Review transcripts, speakers, timestamps, summaries, sentiment, detected moments and supporting evidence.

Quality management

Evaluate interactions using configurable scorecards, automated analysis and human review.

Coaching

Turn conversation evidence and quality findings into focused coaching opportunities.

Voice of customer

Identify recurring customer pain points, emerging themes, service failures and product feedback.

UX and research intelligence

Organise interviews and research conversations, identify patterns and connect findings to source evidence.

Semantic search and retrieval

Search across conversations and retrieve relevant findings using natural-language queries.

Evidence and governance

Connect AI-generated findings to transcripts, speakers, timestamps, context and review decisions.

Workflow and pipeline engine

Move interaction data from source systems through Tessavra analysis and into business applications.

APIs and extensibility

Support authorised integrations, custom workflows, APIs, webhooks and MCP-based access.

Data pipelines and automation

Tessavra can ingest authorised customer interactions from enterprise systems, process those interactions through transcription and structured analysis, and deliver the resulting intelligence into operational applications.

Ingest → Transcribe → Analyse → Review → Deliver

  1. Contact centre to Tessavra

    Ingest recorded calls and associated metadata from approved telephony and contact-centre systems.

  2. Tessavra to Salesforce

    Deliver transcripts, summaries, speaker information, quality results, customer signals and evidence links into Salesforce workflows.

  3. Tessavra to data platforms

    Send approved structured outputs into data warehouses, analytics environments or authorised downstream systems.

  4. Tessavra API and webhooks

    Create custom ingestion, notification and delivery workflows using APIs and webhooks.

  5. Tessavra MCP access

    Allow authorised AI systems and applications to retrieve Tessavra intelligence through controlled interfaces.

These pipelines are productised workflows for moving conversation data into, through and out of Tessavra — not a generic integration platform or consultancy offering. Specific integrations can support or are designed to support the systems above; availability is confirmed through approved integration work rather than assumed.

Common questions

Is Tessavra only a transcription platform?

No. Transcription is one part of the platform. Tessavra also provides interaction analysis, quality management, coaching, voice-of-customer intelligence, research analysis, semantic search, evidence-backed review and workflow automation.

Can Tessavra integrate with Salesforce?

Tessavra is designed to support productised pipelines that can deliver transcripts, summaries, speaker information, quality findings and customer signals into approved Salesforce workflows.

Does Tessavra support customer-support quality assurance?

Yes. Tessavra supports structured interaction review, configurable scorecards, quality analysis, evidence-backed findings, manager review and coaching workflows.

Can Tessavra analyse UX and research interviews?

Yes. Tessavra can organise and analyse recorded research conversations, identify themes and connect findings to the underlying transcript evidence.

How does Tessavra make AI findings reviewable?

Tessavra is designed to connect findings to supporting conversation evidence such as transcript excerpts, speakers, timestamps, context and review decisions.

Who operates Tessavra?

Tessavra is owned and operated by Tessavra Ltd, a wholly owned subsidiary of Talvrix Group Ltd.

How can an organisation evaluate Tessavra?

Use the existing “Request a demo” route and call to action.
