Voice of Customer Intelligence

Understand what customers are saying — at scale, with evidence

Tessavra surfaces recurring customer themes, pain points and feature requests across thousands of support interactions, and links every theme to the supporting conversation evidence — so your team sees patterns without losing customer voices.

The VoC challenge

  1. 01

    Feedback siloed across systems

    Customer signals live in support tickets, call transcripts, chat logs, meeting notes — scattered across tools and teams. No single source of truth for "what are customers telling us?"

  2. 02

    Themes buried in transcripts

    Reading through hundreds of transcripts manually to find recurring complaints or feature requests is time-consuming and inconsistent. Aggregate sentiment scores ("78% positive") don't tell you why customers are frustrated.

  3. 03

    Context lost in summaries

    Traditional text analytics produce keyword clouds or category counts ("47 mentions of 'billing'") but don't show what customers said about billing or which conversations contain those mentions.

How Tessavra surfaces VoC intelligence

  1. 01

    Surface recurring themes at scale

    Tessavra analyzes thousands of customer interactions and surfaces recurring themes — product issues, feature requests, billing complaints, onboarding friction, competitive mentions — without requiring manual tagging.

  2. 02

    Link every theme to supporting evidence

    Each theme includes a list of supporting conversation examples, with direct links to the transcript lines where customers expressed that theme. CX teams click through to see the full customer voice in context.

  3. 03

    Organize feedback for action

    Themes are organized by topic, frequency and urgency. CX teams, product managers and support leaders see which issues are growing, which are resolved, and which require immediate attention.

From conversations to customer intelligence

  1. 01

    Capture

    Tessavra ingests customer interactions from contact centers, CRM systems, support platforms, meeting tools or uploaded files.

  2. 02

    Analyze

    Conversations are analyzed for recurring themes, customer pain points, feature requests and positive signals.

What Tessavra surfaces

Product Issues & Bugs

Recurring complaints about broken features, confusing workflows or technical problems. Every issue links to customer descriptions in their own words.

Feature Requests

What customers are asking for — new capabilities, workflow improvements, integration requests. See which requests appear most frequently and which customers are asking.

Pain Points & Friction

Onboarding confusion, billing disputes, policy frustration, support handoff issues — surface where customers are struggling, with conversation evidence.

Competitive Mentions

When customers mention competing products, switching considerations or feature comparisons. Understand what customers value in alternatives.

Positive Signals

What's working well — praise for specific agents, features customers love, successful onboarding experiences. Not just complaints.

Churn Risk Indicators

Frustration patterns, repeated issues, cancellation threats — surface at-risk customers before they leave, with evidence for account teams to review.

Filter, prioritize, route

  • Filter by topic, team or time — view themes by product area, support team, customer segment or time period, and track how themes evolve over time (growing, stable, declining)
  • Prioritize by frequency and urgency — see which themes appear most often, which are escalating, and which require immediate action
  • Route to the right teams — share themes with product, engineering, support ops or account teams, with supporting conversation evidence so they see the customer voice, not a summary of a summary
  • Track resolution — mark themes as "investigating," "fixed" or "closed," and track when customer mentions decline after a product update or process change
