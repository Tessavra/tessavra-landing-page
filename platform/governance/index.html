Evidence & Governance

Evidence-backed decisions, audit-ready workflows

Tessavra links every quality finding, customer signal and AI-generated result to the underlying conversation evidence — and logs every review, approval and override — so your quality decisions are reviewable, auditable and defensible.

Request a demo See governance in action

The governance challenge

  1. 01

    Black-box scores without evidence

    Automated quality tools produce scores and findings, but don't show why — making it impossible for managers to verify results or for auditors to trace decisions back to source interactions.

  2. 02

    No audit trail for decisions

    When a manager overrides a quality score, approves a coaching action or disputes a finding, that decision often lives in email or untracked conversations — not in a system audit log.

  3. 03

    Ungoverned AI outputs

    AI-generated summaries, sentiment scores and theme labels lack traceability to the underlying conversation — creating compliance risk when those outputs inform customer-facing decisions or regulatory reporting.

How Tessavra provides governance

  1. 01

    Link every result to conversation evidence

    Every quality finding, customer signal, theme and AI-generated output includes a direct link to the specific transcript line, timestamp or conversational turn that produced it — so managers and auditors can verify results against the source interaction.

  2. 02

    Log every review and decision

    Manager reviews, approvals, overrides, coaching actions and calibration outcomes are logged with timestamps, user IDs and decision rationale — creating an audit trail for compliance, quality tracking and operational review.

  3. 03

    Configure approval gates and policy guardrails

    Require manager approval before delivering high-stakes outputs (customer communications, escalations, coaching actions). Configure policy rules that flag interactions for review (prohibited language, script deviations, compliance triggers).

Review, approve, audit — with full evidence context

  1. 01

    Flag for review

    Tessavra evaluates interactions and surfaces findings that require manager review (low scores, policy breaches, high-stakes signals).

  2. 02

    Review with evidence

    Managers click through to see the full transcript, verify the finding against conversation context, and decide whether to confirm, override or escalate.

  3. 03

    Approve or override

    Managers confirm findings, override incorrect results (with rationale), or escalate for additional review. Every decision is logged.

  4. 04

    Audit trail

    All reviews, approvals, overrides and outcomes are tracked — who reviewed what, when, and what decisions were made — for compliance audits, performance tracking and operational review.

Who reviewed what, when, and why

  • Decision logs — Track every manager review, approval, override, coaching action and calibration outcome — with timestamps, user IDs and decision rationale.
  • Access logs — Track who accessed which interactions, when, and what actions they took — for compliance audits and internal security review.
  • Pipeline run logs — Track workflow executions, data flows, delivery success/failure — so operations teams see what data moved where, and auditors can verify compliance with data handling policies.
  • Retention compliance — Configure retention policies that automatically archive or delete interactions and findings according to your regulatory requirements — with audit trails for retention actions.
  • Export for compliance — Export audit logs, decision records and evidence chains for compliance reporting, regulatory audits or internal quality reviews.

Configure rules, flag issues, require approval

Policy rule configuration

Define rules that flag interactions for review — prohibited language, script deviations, compliance triggers, escalation patterns, churn risk indicators.

Automatic flagging

Tessavra surfaces interactions that violate configured policies, with evidence links to the specific conversational turns where the issue occurred.

Approval gates

Require manager approval before delivering high-stakes outputs (customer communications, agent performance reports, regulatory reporting data).

Override tracking

When managers override a policy flag or approve a borderline result, the override reason and approver are logged for audit.

Outcome: Configurable governance that reflects your organization's policies — not a one-size-fits-all compliance template.

Key capabilities

  • Evidence linking — Every finding links to transcript evidence for manager verification
  • Manager approval workflows — Require human review before delivering high-stakes outputs
  • Audit trails — Log every review, approval, override, access, pipeline run
