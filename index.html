The problem

Quality review rarely keeps pace with conversation volume.

Most support organizations can only sample a fraction of calls and tickets for quality review. Scores get recorded, but the reasoning behind them — the specific words, tone, or moment that triggered a rating — is usually lost by the time anyone needs it again.

  1. 01

    Low review coverage

    Manual QA reaches only a small share of conversations, so systemic issues often surface first in complaints or churn — not in the QA queue.

  2. 02

    Scores without evidence

    A score on its own means little without the transcript line that produced it, especially when an agent or a customer disputes the result.

  3. 03

    Insights stay siloed

    Patterns worth sharing with product or UX teams rarely leave the spreadsheet or dashboard where a QA reviewer first captured them.

How it works

Every finding is a citation, not just a score.

Tessavra reads support conversations across voice, chat, and email, and evaluates them against the rubric your quality team already uses. Each finding is linked directly to the transcript excerpt, timestamp, and speaker that produced it.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Conversations are ingested from your support systems in their original form.

  2. 02

    Evaluate

    Rubric criteria are scored using the transcript itself as the only source of truth.

  3. 03

    Cite

    Each score links to the exact evidence: the line, timestamp, and speaker.

  4. 04

    Review

    A human reviewer can confirm, adjust, or challenge any finding directly against its citation.

Support QA & coaching

Coach from the transcript, not the summary.

Run your existing rubric across every conversation instead of a manual sample. Flagged findings route to a review queue where team leads can confirm the evidence, add coaching notes, and close the loop with the agent — without leaving the original transcript.

  • Custom rubric scoring aligned to your existing QA process
  • Automatic routing of flagged conversations to the right reviewer
  • Coaching notes attached directly to the evidence that prompted them
  • Agent- and team-level trend views over time

Calibration & governance

Keep automated scoring accountable to human judgment.

Automated evaluation is only trustworthy if it's regularly checked against how your own reviewers would score the same conversation. Tessavra supports structured calibration sessions where reviewers score a shared sample, compare results, and adjust rubric interpretation together — with a recorded history of every calibration decision.

Security & data control

Your conversation data stays under your control.

Support conversations often contain sensitive customer information. Tessavra is designed around that reality from the ground up.

Role-based access

Access to transcripts, findings, and calibration history is governed by role-based permissions.

Audit logging

Every view or edit of a finding is recorded in an audit log tied to the reviewer who made it.

Configurable retention

Data retention and deletion schedules follow the policy your organization sets, not a fixed default.

Data processing terms

Data processing terms are available for review before any evaluation of your conversations begins.
