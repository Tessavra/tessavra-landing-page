What is Tessavra?

Tessavra is a conversation intelligence platform for enterprise customer support, quality assurance, customer experience and research teams.

Unlike traditional analytics tools, Tessavra links every quality finding, customer signal and insight to the exact transcript evidence that produced it — making results reviewable, auditable and actionable.

Built for high-volume, regulated support organizations where quality, compliance and evidence-backed coaching are essential.

Every finding links to the evidence

Tessavra analyzes conversations against your configured rubric, scorecards and research questions. Every finding, score and theme is linked to the specific transcript line, timestamp or conversational turn that produced it.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Ingest interactions from contact centers, CRM systems, meeting tools or uploaded files.

  2. 02

    Transcribe & Structure

    Tessavra transcribes conversations, identifies speakers, and segments them into conversational turns.

  3. 03

    Analyze

    Evaluate conversations against your configured quality rubric, research questions or analysis frameworks.

  4. 04

    Link

    Every score, finding and theme is linked to the specific transcript line, timestamp or conversational turn that produced it.

  5. 05

    Review

    Your teams review findings with full conversation context — no black-box scores, no unsupported claims.
