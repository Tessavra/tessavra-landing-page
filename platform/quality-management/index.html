Quality Management

From 2% sampling to 100% quality coverage

Tessavra evaluates every customer interaction against your quality rubric and links every score to the exact transcript evidence — so QA teams can review findings, coach with evidence, and track performance at scale.

Request a demo See quality management in action

The quality coverage problem

  1. 01

    Low coverage, high blind spots

    Traditional QA teams manually review 1–2% of interactions. The other 98% goes unmonitored — where policy breaches, coaching opportunities and customer issues hide.

  2. 02

    Scores without evidence

    Automated QA tools produce scores but don't show why — making it hard for agents to improve and managers to trust the results.

  3. 03

    Inconsistent calibration

    When QA reviewers apply scorecards manually, scoring consistency varies. Without shared evidence, calibration becomes subjective debate.

How Tessavra solves it

  1. 01

    Evaluate 100% of interactions

    Tessavra analyzes every customer conversation against your configured quality scorecard — not a 2% sample. Surface the interactions that need human review and archive the rest with full audit trails.

  2. 02

    Link every score to transcript evidence

    Every quality finding links to the exact transcript line where that issue occurred. Reviewers see the evidence, verify the finding, and decide whether to confirm or override.

  3. 03

    Calibrate with shared evidence

    Managers and QA teams review the same transcript evidence when discussing scoring consistency. Calibration sessions become evidence-backed discussions, not opinion debates.

Manager review queues, evidence-backed coaching

Tessavra ingests interactions from your contact center or CRM system, transcribes them, and evaluates them against your quality scorecard. Interactions with low scores, policy breaches or coaching opportunities are surfaced in manager review queues.

  1. 01

    Capture & Evaluate

    Tessavra ingests interactions and evaluates them against your quality scorecard.

  2. 02

    Flag for Review

    Interactions with low scores, policy breaches or coaching opportunities are surfaced in manager review queues.

  3. 03

    Review with Evidence

    Managers click through to see the full transcript, verify the findings, and decide whether to confirm, override or coach.

  4. 04

    Coach & Track

    Evidence-backed coaching feedback is delivered to agents. Performance trends are tracked over time.

Define what good looks like

Build scorecards with weighted criteria that reflect your support standards — acknowledgment, empathy, policy adherence, issue resolution, professional tone. Tessavra evaluates interactions against each scorecard criterion and links the result to transcript evidence.

  • Configurable criteria that reflect your support standards
  • Evidence-linked evaluation for every criterion
  • Multi-scorecard support for different teams, channels or interaction types
  • Manager override with audit-logged override reasons

Calibration with shared evidence

QA managers and reviewers work through a set of interactions together, applying the scorecard criteria and discussing scoring decisions. Instead of debating whether an agent sounded empathetic, reviewers see the exact transcript lines and decide together whether the scorecard criterion was met.

  • Calibration sessions with shared transcript evidence
  • Calibration logs that track scoring variance and areas of disagreement
  • Audit trail for every scoring decision, manager override and calibration outcome
