The Salesforce challenge

Conversation intelligence lives outside Salesforce

Sales, support and customer success teams work in Salesforce, but conversation transcripts, quality results and customer signals live in separate tools. Switching between systems slows workflows and buries insights.

Manual data entry or no data at all

Teams manually copy call summaries, customer issues or quality notes into Salesforce — time-consuming, inconsistent, and often skipped. Without automated delivery, conversation intelligence never reaches CRM records.

No evidence for customer claims

When a customer signal (churn risk, feature request, competitive mention) is noted in Salesforce, there's often no link back to the conversation where that signal appeared — making it hard to verify or act on.

How Tessavra enriches Salesforce

Enrich Salesforce records automatically

Tessavra delivers transcripts, summaries, quality results, customer signals and evidence-backed findings into Salesforce records — Cases, Accounts, Contacts, Opportunities, custom objects — without manual data entry.

Link intelligence to conversation evidence

Every quality finding, customer signal and AI-generated summary delivered to Salesforce includes a link back to the source transcript and the specific conversational moment that produced it.

Keep teams in Salesforce

Sales, support and customer success teams see conversation intelligence in the records they're already reviewing — no need to switch tools, log into separate dashboards, or hunt for transcripts.

What Tessavra delivers to Salesforce

Transcripts & summaries

Full conversation transcripts, speaker-identified and timestamped. AI-generated summaries for quick review.

Quality results

Quality scores from configured scorecards, with evidence links to the transcript lines that produced each score.

Customer signals

Churn risk indicators, feature requests, competitive mentions, escalation patterns — surfaced and delivered to relevant Salesforce records.

Evidence links

Every delivered finding includes a link back to the source transcript and the specific conversational moment — no unsupported claims.

Speaker & metadata

Speaker identification (customer, agent, manager), call duration, channel (phone, chat, meeting), timestamp.

Theme & sentiment

Recurring customer themes, sentiment indicators — linked to supporting conversation evidence.

Integration capabilities

  • Automated record enrichment — Deliver Tessavra intelligence into Salesforce records without manual data entry
  • Evidence-linked findings — Every delivered signal includes a link back to the source transcript
  • Multi-object support — Enrich Cases, Accounts, Contacts, Opportunities, custom objects
  • Configurable delivery rules — Choose what data goes where, when, and under what conditions
  • Scheduled & real-time delivery — Batch updates or real-time enrichment
  • Audit trails — Track what data was delivered to which Salesforce records, when
  • Governed workflows — Approval gates, review queues, manager override tracked in Salesforce
  • Two-way sync — Pull interaction metadata from Salesforce, deliver intelligence back

Productised pipelines, not one-click install

Configured workflows: Tessavra's Salesforce integration is built as configured pipelines that capture interaction data, route it through analysis, and deliver structured outputs to your Salesforce instance.

Implementation scope: Setup includes: Salesforce object mapping, field mapping, delivery rules, approval workflows, error handling. Typically involves collaboration between Tessavra support, your Salesforce admin, and operations teams.

Ongoing management: Operations teams monitor pipeline runs, adjust delivery rules, and maintain integration health via Tessavra's workflow dashboard.

Specific Salesforce workflows and object mappings are configured during implementation.
