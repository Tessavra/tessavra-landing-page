Product & UX Research

From recorded research sessions to evidence-backed insights

Tessavra transcribes research interviews, surfaces themes across sessions, and links every finding to transcript evidence — so research teams spend less time on manual transcription and more time on analysis and synthesis.

Request a demo See research workflows in action

The research team challenge

  1. 01

    Transcription bottleneck

    Researchers spend hours transcribing recorded interviews, typing up session notes, and organizing recordings before analysis can begin. This delays insights and limits how many research sessions teams can run.

  2. 02

    Manual theme coding

    Identifying recurring themes across dozens of research sessions requires manually coding transcripts, tagging quotes, and organizing findings in spreadsheets — time-consuming and inconsistent.

  3. 03

    Evidence lost in synthesis

    By the time research insights reach a product brief or stakeholder presentation, the supporting customer quotes and conversation context are often summarized away — making it hard to verify findings or explore nuance.

How Tessavra helps research teams

  1. 01

    Transcribe research sessions automatically

    Upload recorded interviews, usability sessions or customer discovery conversations. Tessavra transcribes them, identifies speakers (researcher, participant), and organizes by conversational turn — ready for analysis.

  2. 02

    Surface themes across sessions

    Tessavra analyzes transcripts for recurring themes — user pain points, feature preferences, workflow friction, positive signals — and surfaces patterns across multiple research sessions without requiring manual coding.

  3. 03

    Link insights to transcript evidence

    Every theme, finding and insight includes direct links to the transcript lines where participants expressed that idea. Researchers cite evidence in reports, and stakeholders click through to see the participant voice in context.

What Tessavra surfaces for researchers

Beyond transcription — structured research signals across sessions.

User Pain Points

Workflow friction, confusing UI, onboarding challenges — surface where users struggle, with supporting quotes from research sessions.

Feature Preferences

What users want, which features they value most, which capabilities they'd pay for. See patterns across multiple participants.

Usability Issues

Task completion failures, navigation confusion, error recovery problems — linked to the exact session moments where users encountered those issues.

Behavioral Patterns

How users actually work (vs. how you expected them to). Surface unexpected workflows, workarounds and mental models.

Competitive Context

When participants mention competing products, current tools or switching considerations. Understand what users value in alternatives.

Positive Signals

What's working well — features users love, successful task completions, delightful moments. Not just problems.

Organize sessions, surface patterns

Manage dozens of research sessions without losing context.

  • Project-based organization

    Group research sessions by project, study or research question. Track which sessions belong to which initiative.

  • Filter by participant, topic or finding

    View themes by user segment, product area or research question. Track how findings evolve across multiple studies.

  • Cross-session patterns

    See which themes appear across multiple participants, which are unique to a segment, and which are consistent over time.

  • Collaborate with stakeholders

    Share findings with product, design or engineering teams — with supporting transcript quotes so they hear the user voice, not a researcher's summary.
