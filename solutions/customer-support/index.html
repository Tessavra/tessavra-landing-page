Customer Support

Support teams that scale quality, not just volume

Tessavra helps support leaders evaluate 100% of interactions, coach agents with evidence-backed feedback, and surface recurring issues — without hiring more QA reviewers.

Request a demo See quality management in action

The support team challenge

  1. 01

    Quality blind spots

    Support leaders manually review 1–2% of interactions. The other 98% goes unmonitored — where coaching opportunities, policy breaches and customer frustration hide.

  2. 02

    Reactive coaching without evidence

    Coaching conversations rely on manager memory or cherry-picked examples. Agents receive feedback weeks after the interaction, without seeing the specific moment that needs improvement.

  3. 03

    Recurring issues buried in volume

    The same customer complaints, knowledge gaps and workflow friction appear across hundreds of tickets — but support leaders don't see the pattern until it's a crisis.

How Tessavra helps support teams

  1. 01

    Evaluate 100% of interactions

    Tessavra analyzes every customer conversation against your quality rubric and surfaces the interactions that need manager review — flagged issues, low scores, policy breaches, coaching opportunities.

  2. 02

    Coach agents with evidence

    Instead of "you need to show more empathy," coaches share the exact transcript moment where the agent could have acknowledged the customer's concern — with full conversation context.

  3. 03

    Surface recurring issues proactively

    Tessavra identifies patterns across thousands of interactions — recurring customer complaints, knowledge gaps, workflow friction — so support leaders fix systemic issues before they escalate.

Quality management at scale

From 2% sampling to 100% coverage

Every support interaction is evaluated against your configured quality scorecard. Tessavra surfaces the interactions that need human review — so your managers spend their time on the conversations that matter, not on random sampling.

  • Automatic evaluation — every interaction evaluated against your scorecard; the ones that need review are surfaced
  • Manager review queues — flagged interactions organized by priority: policy breaches, low scores, escalations, coaching opportunities
  • Evidence-linked findings — every quality issue links to the specific transcript line where it occurred
  • Performance tracking — quality trends over time, by team, by agent, by scorecard criterion

Evidence-backed coaching

Coach with evidence, not opinions

Coaching feedback lands best when it's specific, timely and verifiable. With Tessavra, agents see the exact conversation moment that needs improvement — not a generic summary delivered weeks later.

  • Specific feedback — the exact conversation moment, with manager notes and guidance, instead of generic coaching
  • Timely delivery — feedback shortly after the interaction, while the conversation is still fresh
  • Evidence context — agents click through to the full transcript and review their response in context
  • Track improvement — monitor which coaching actions are working and where agents need additional support

Surfacing recurring issues

Find patterns before they become crises

The same complaints, knowledge gaps and workflow friction repeat across hundreds of interactions. Tessavra groups them into themes — with the conversation evidence behind each one — so support leaders fix systemic issues before they escalate.

  • Theme surfacing — recurring complaints, knowledge gaps and workflow friction across thousands of interactions
  • Evidence for each theme — supporting conversation examples, in customers' own words
  • Root cause analysis — distinguish product bugs, knowledge gaps, policy problems and workflow failures
  • Route to the right teams — share themes with product, engineering, operations or training, with evidence attached
