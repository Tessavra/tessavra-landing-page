Security and data control for regulated support

Tessavra is designed for high-volume, regulated support organizations where quality decisions must be auditable, data handling must be governed, and access must be controlled.

Tessavra provides the security and governance capabilities that support compliance posture — access controls, audit trails, retention policies, encrypted data handling. We do not claim SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR certification or other compliance certifications.

If your organization requires specific compliance frameworks, data processing terms or security documentation, contact us to discuss your requirements.

Your data, your controls

Configurable retention policies: Define how long interaction data, transcripts, quality results and analysis findings are retained. Automated retention enforcement with audit logs.

Encrypted storage and transit: Interaction data, transcripts and analysis results are encrypted at rest and in transit.

Data isolation: Customer data is isolated per organization. No cross-customer data sharing or training on customer data without explicit agreement.

Data export and deletion: Customers can export their data or request deletion per retention policy and legal requirements.

Configurable data handling: Choose which interaction data fields are captured, stored, analyzed or delivered to downstream systems.

Control who sees what

Role-based permissions: Define roles (QA manager, QA analyst, support lead, operations admin) with specific permissions for viewing interactions, reviewing findings, approving workflows, configuring pipelines.

Team-level access: Restrict access to interactions by team, channel, or customer segment. QA analysts see only the interactions assigned to their review scope.

User authentication: Secure authentication with session management, password policies, multi-factor authentication support (where available).

Access audit logs: Track who accessed which interactions, when, and what actions they took — for compliance audits and internal security review.

Audit trails for every action

Access logs: Track who accessed which interactions, transcripts, quality results — with timestamps and user IDs.

Review and decision logs: Track manager reviews, quality score confirmations, overrides, coaching actions — with decision rationale.

Delivery logs: Track what data was delivered to which downstream systems (Salesforce, data warehouse, alerts) and when.

Retention and deletion logs: Track retention policy enforcement, data archival, deletion actions — for compliance reporting.

Pipeline execution logs: Track workflow runs, data flows, success/failure rates — for operations monitoring and compliance audits.

Export for compliance: Export audit logs for regulatory reporting, internal audits or compliance reviews.

Learn more about evidence and governance

AI outputs linked to evidence, reviewed by humans

Evidence-linked AI outputs: Every AI-generated quality score, summary, theme or customer signal includes a direct link to the transcript evidence that produced it. No black-box outputs.

Human review workflows: AI-generated findings are surfaced for manager review. Managers verify results against conversation context and confirm, override or escalate.

Manager override tracking: When managers override an AI-generated result, the override reason and approver are logged for audit.

Model behavior transparency: Tessavra documents what AI models do (transcription, quality evaluation, theme surfacing) and how outputs are generated (analysis against configured rubrics, theme clustering).

No autonomous actions: Tessavra does not take customer-facing actions autonomously. AI-generated findings are surfaced for human review and decision-making.
