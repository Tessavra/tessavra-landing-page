What is conversation intelligence?

Conversation intelligence is the practice of analyzing customer interactions at scale to surface quality findings, customer signals and recurring themes — and connecting those findings to the underlying conversation evidence.

Traditional analytics tools produce scores and sentiment labels without showing why a conversation received that score or where a customer expressed frustration. Tessavra links every finding to the transcript line that produced it, making results reviewable, auditable and actionable.

Every finding links to the evidence

Tessavra analyzes conversations against your configured rubric, scorecards and research questions. Every finding, score and theme is linked to the specific transcript line, timestamp or conversational turn that produced it.

Core capabilities

Transcription & Speaker Identification

Accurate transcription of voice calls, with speaker diarization. Handles multi-speaker conversations, accents, crosstalk. Supports uploaded audio files, telephony integrations, meeting platform recordings. Turn recorded interactions into searchable, analyzable transcripts.

Turn-by-Turn Analysis

Analyze conversations at the turn level, not just aggregate scores. Identify which specific conversational turns contain quality issues, customer pain points, policy breaches or positive signals. Pin findings to the exact moment in the conversation.

Semantic Search

Search across conversation history by meaning, not just keywords. Find interactions where a customer expressed frustration, where an agent offered a discount, or where a specific product feature was discussed. Surface relevant conversations for review, coaching or analysis.

Evidence Linking & Audit Trail

Every finding, score and theme includes a direct link to the transcript evidence that produced it. Reviewers click through to see the full conversation context. Audit logs track who reviewed what, when, and what decisions were made. Reviewable, auditable intelligence.

Browse and filter interaction history

A ledger-style interface for browsing all captured interactions. Filter by date, speaker, quality result, customer signal, team or topic. QA managers review flagged interactions; CX leads find customer complaints about a specific product feature; researchers locate interviews with a particular user segment.

Find conversations by meaning, not just keywords

Semantic search across transcript content. Query in natural language ("customer was confused about billing") and surface relevant interactions. Support leads find similar issues for calibration; product teams research specific feature mentions; compliance teams audit policy adherence.
