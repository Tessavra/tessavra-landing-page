How Tessavra turns conversations into evidence-backed intelligence

A practical guide to how Tessavra captures interactions, analyzes them against your quality rubric, links findings to transcript evidence, and delivers intelligence to your teams.

Request a demo Explore the platform

Four stages from conversation to intelligence

Tessavra is a conversation intelligence platform that evaluates customer interactions against your quality rubric, surfaces themes and signals, and links every finding to the exact transcript evidence that produced it. The workflow has four stages:

1. Capture — Ingest interaction data from contact centers, CRM systems, meeting platforms

2. Evaluate — Analyze conversations against your configured quality rubric, scorecards, research questions

3. Review — Managers and teams review findings with full conversation context and evidence links

4. Act — Deliver intelligence into business applications (Salesforce, data warehouse, support systems, alerts)

Analyze conversations, link findings to evidence

Transcription: Voice calls are transcribed, speakers are identified, conversations are segmented into turns

Quality evaluation: Interactions are evaluated against your configured quality scorecard — acknowledgment, empathy, policy adherence, issue resolution, professional tone

Theme surfacing: Recurring customer themes, pain points and feature requests are surfaced across thousands of interactions

Customer signal detection: Churn risk indicators, escalation patterns, competitive mentions, positive signals

Evidence linking: Every quality finding, theme and signal is linked to the specific transcript line, timestamp or conversational turn that produced it

No black-box scores, no unsupported claims — every result includes the evidence for manager review.

Managers review findings with full evidence context

Flagged for review: Tessavra surfaces interactions that need manager review — low quality scores, policy breaches, high-stakes customer signals, coaching opportunities

Review with evidence: Managers click through to see the full transcript, verify findings against conversation context, and decide whether to confirm, override or escalate

Manager override: Managers can override Tessavra's evaluation when context requires human judgment — with override reasons tracked in audit logs

Calibration: QA teams review the same interactions together, applying scorecard criteria with shared evidence to align scoring consistency

Coaching delivery: Evidence-backed coaching feedback is delivered to agents — "Here's the moment where you could have acknowledged the customer's concern"

Human oversight, not automation. Findings are reviewable, auditable and governed by manager decision-making.
