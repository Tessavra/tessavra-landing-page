Quality Assurance

QA teams that scale coverage without scaling headcount

Tessavra evaluates 100% of support interactions against your quality rubric, links every score to transcript evidence, and surfaces the conversations that need QA review — so your team focuses on high-value analysis, not manual sampling.

The QA team challenge

  1. 01

    Sampling limits coverage

    QA teams manually review 1–2% of interactions. Scaling coverage means hiring more reviewers or accepting blind spots where policy breaches and quality issues hide.

  2. 02

    Scoring consistency varies

    When QA analysts apply scorecards manually, scoring consistency differs. Without shared evidence, calibration sessions become subjective debates about what "good" looks like.

  3. 03

    Audit trails are incomplete

    Regulated support environments require evidence trails for quality decisions. Manual QA processes often lack audit logs for who reviewed what, when, and why scores were assigned.

How Tessavra helps QA teams

  1. 01

    Scale to 100% coverage

    Tessavra evaluates every interaction against your configured quality scorecard and surfaces the conversations that need QA review — policy breaches, low scores, edge cases, coaching opportunities. QA teams review findings with full evidence context, not random samples.

  2. 02

    Calibrate with shared evidence

    Instead of debating whether an agent "sounded professional," QA teams review the exact transcript lines together and decide whether scorecard criteria were met. Calibration sessions become evidence-backed discussions, not opinion debates.

  3. 03

    Maintain audit-ready workflows

    Every quality score, QA review, manager override and calibration outcome is logged with timestamps, reviewer IDs and decision rationale — creating an audit trail for compliance reporting and operational review.

QA workflow

Flag → Review → Confirm → Audit

Tessavra evaluates 100% of interactions against your quality scorecard, then routes the conversations that need QA review into a prioritized queue — so analysts spend their time on high-value review, not random sampling.

  • Automatic evaluation — every interaction evaluated against your scorecard; low scores, policy breaches and edge cases flagged for QA review
  • QA review queue — flagged interactions organized by priority, each with the quality score, flagged criteria and evidence links
  • Evidence-backed review — analysts open the full transcript, verify findings against conversation context, and confirm or override Tessavra's evaluation
  • Audit log — every review, confirmation, override and scoring decision logged: who reviewed what, when, and what rationale was provided

Calibration with shared evidence

Calibration sessions backed by evidence, not opinions

Calibration is where scoring consistency is won or lost. With Tessavra, QA managers and analysts review the same set of interactions together — with every discussion grounded in the exact transcript moments under review.

  • Shared transcript review — QA managers and analysts apply scorecard criteria to the same interactions and discuss scoring decisions together
  • Evidence-linked discussion — instead of abstract debates about "professionalism" or "empathy," sessions focus on specific transcript moments: "Did the agent acknowledge the customer's concern in line 14?"
  • Track scoring variance — calibration logs show where reviewers agree, where they diverge, and which scorecard criteria need clearer definitions
  • Refine scorecards — use calibration data to improve scorecard wording, add examples, or split ambiguous criteria into more specific measures

Audit-ready quality management

Audit trails for every quality decision

  • Review logs — track which QA analysts reviewed which interactions, when, and what scoring decisions were made
  • Override tracking — when analysts override Tessavra's evaluation, the override reason and approver are logged
  • Calibration records — track calibration session results, scoring variance, and refinements to scorecard criteria
  • Evidence preservation — source transcripts, quality scores and decision logs are retained according to your configured retention policies
  • Compliance reporting — export audit logs, decision records and evidence chains for compliance audits, regulatory reporting or internal quality reviews
