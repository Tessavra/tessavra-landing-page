Workflow & Pipeline Engine

Automate conversation intelligence workflows — with visibility and control

Tessavra's workflow engine moves interaction data from contact centers, CRM systems and meeting platforms through analysis and into business applications — with configured pipelines, approval gates and audit trails at every stage.

Request a demo See workflows in action

The workflow challenge

  1. 01

    Manual data movement

    Interaction data lives in contact centers, CRM systems and meeting platforms. Analysis results need to reach support teams, data warehouses, operational systems — but moving that data manually is time-consuming and error-prone.

  2. 02

    Disconnected systems

    Quality findings, customer signals and conversation intelligence sit in one tool. Sales, support and analytics teams work in different systems. Without integration, insights don't reach the teams who need them.

  3. 03

    Governance and visibility gaps

    Automated data pipelines without visibility or control become black boxes. Teams need to see what data is moving, where it's going, who approved it, and when pipelines fail.

How Tessavra's workflow engine solves it

  1. 01

    Configure governed pipelines

    Build workflows that capture interaction data from source systems, route it through Tessavra analysis (quality evaluation, theme surfacing, signal detection), and deliver structured outputs to business applications — with configured rules, approval gates and error handling.

  2. 02

    Maintain visibility and control

    Track pipeline runs, view data flow logs, monitor success/failure rates. Operations teams see what data is moving, where it's going, and when pipelines require attention.

  3. 03

    Deliver intelligence to business applications

    Push Tessavra analysis results into Salesforce, support platforms, data warehouses, operational dashboards — so teams act on conversation intelligence in the tools they already use.

Capture → Evaluate → Review → Act

Capture

Ingest interaction data from contact center platforms, CRM systems, meeting tools, chat platforms or uploaded files.

Evaluate

Route interactions through Tessavra analysis — quality scorecards, theme surfacing, customer signal detection, policy checks.

Review

Manager review queues for flagged findings, approval gates for high-stakes results, calibration workflows for scoring consistency.

Act

Deliver Tessavra results into business applications — Salesforce records, support tickets, data warehouse tables, operational dashboards, alert systems.

  1. 01

    Capture — details & governance

    Details: Scheduled pipelines, event-driven triggers, API ingestion, webhook receivers.

    Governance: Retention rules, data classification, access controls applied at ingestion.

  2. 02

    Evaluate — details & governance

    Details: Configurable analysis rules, scorecard assignment, evidence linking, enrichment with metadata.

    Governance: Analysis audit logs, evidence traceability, model behavior records.

  3. 03

    Review — details & governance

    Details: Review queues, approval workflows, manager override, evidence-backed decisions.

    Governance: Who reviewed what, when, and what decisions were made — logged for audit.

  4. 04

    Act — details & governance

    Details: API delivery, webhook dispatch, scheduled exports, real-time notifications.

    Governance: Delivery logs, success/failure tracking, retry handling, data lineage.

Governed workflows, not black boxes

  • Pipeline run logs — track every pipeline execution: what data was captured, what analysis ran, what outputs were delivered, when pipelines failed
  • Approval gates — configure manager review and approval requirements for high-stakes workflows (e.g., customer-facing communications, compliance-sensitive outputs)
  • Evidence traceability — every delivered finding includes links back to the source interaction and the analysis that produced it, with no unsupported claims
  • Error handling & retry — pipelines handle transient failures gracefully, retry delivery on error, and alert operations teams when manual intervention is required
  • Access controls — configure who can create, modify, run or approve pipelines, with role-based permissions and audit logs
